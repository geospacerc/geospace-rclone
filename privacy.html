Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

About this application

Geospace rclone is a private application used for personal Google Drive synchronization through rclone.

Information accessed

The application may request access to Google Drive data in order to allow rclone to synchronize files with Google Drive.

The application does not intentionally collect, sell, or share personal information with third parties.

Use of Google data

Google Drive data accessed through this application is used solely for the intended synchronization and backup operations.

The application does not use Google Drive data for advertising, profiling, or any unrelated purpose.

Data storage

Authentication credentials and tokens are stored locally on the systems where rclone is configured. They are not intentionally transmitted to or stored by this website.

Contact

For questions about this application, please contact the developer using the contact information associated with the Google Cloud project.
